Fixed fee, quoted up front · 10 business days

We find where developers and their coding agents stall on your platform, then give you the plan to fix it.

  • A cold run of your quickstart by an engineer and by an AI coding agent, with every failure point logged
  • A friction report on docs, onboarding, and support channels, ranked by how many builders each issue stops
  • A review of your hackathons, programs, and community against the numbers we've hit before
  • A metrics baseline you can track every month after we leave
  • A live readout with your team and a 90-day plan

From you: one technical contact, access to docs and repos, and two hours of founder time. The full fee credits toward any engagement booked within 30 days.
